 FB 1 is the basic metal carbonate MCO3•M(OH)2.
 FB 2 is a solution containing hydrochloric acid, HCl , and MCl 2, prepared using FB 1 as follows.
 ● 22.50 g of FB 1, MCO3•M(OH)2, is weighed out.
